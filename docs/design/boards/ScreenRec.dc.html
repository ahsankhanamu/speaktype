Step 5 · at your first session: Screen Recording

Asked once, at the moment it's needed, never during onboarding. Choosing "Not now" still starts the session with voice and copied text.

Let SpeakType see what you draw on

To turn your marks into screenshots, macOS needs you to allow Screen Recording. SpeakType only captures the moment you draw, never video, and it stays on this Mac.

  1. Click Open System Settings
  2. Turn on SpeakType
  3. Come back. Your session starts automatically.
